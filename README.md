# Boltzmann AI Research Agent Hackathon 🤖
## Designing De Novo Protein candidates to mimic the 6s RNA regulatory interface of σ70🧬

- The goal of the work was to obtain protein candidates who could mimic the region of σ70 contacted
contacted by 6s RNA.
- Obtained the 6s RNA-σ70/ RNAP complex from PDB (PDB ID:5VT0)
- Performed docking, analysed interface interaction and obtained 8 candidates who partially mimic the region

 ## Tools used ⚙️ 
- MegaDock
- PRODIGY

## What i learnt
- Structural Bioinformatics
- Protein- protein interaction
- Molecular docking
